SafeBite

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Last updated November 12, 2026 · Qupid, LLC (“we”, “us”)

This policy covers SafeBite for iPhone and SafeBite for Android. Where the two work differently, it says which is which.

The short version

  • SafeBite has no accounts. We never receive your name, your email, or the names and ages of the people in your household.
  • Your household, your Kitchen list and your history stay on your phone.
  • The following may leave your phone: a photo if you choose Google reading (you can read it on-device instead), the alert groups your phone joins (held by Google’s messaging service against an install code, never a name), an anonymous count when an alert arrives or is opened, and, when you use cloud scans, scan counts plus purchase information to enforce allowances (on iPhone, signed Apple purchase information; on Android, a hashed device identifier and, for SafeBite+, the Google Play purchase token).
  • No advertising networks, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting software, no location access. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

1. What stays on your phone

These are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. We cannot see them.

  • Your household: the names you type, whether each profile is an adult, a child or a pet, a child’s age if you enter one, allergens, pet type and your state. An older app version may also have saved an unused alert-strictness answer.
  • Your Kitchen: the products, brands and kinds of food you watch, and a small cropped copy of the photo you added a product with.
  • Recalls you follow and recalls you marked as dealt with.
  • The last 20 checks: the brand, product name and barcode that were read, and any error, kept to help you and us diagnose a failed check. They are never sent to us.
  • Settings such as your alert switches, and a copy of the public recall list and its pictures so the app opens quickly.
  • A small file the app shares with its own widget and notification components on the same phone, holding the household first names, allergens, pet names and watched items, so an alert can say why it is yours. It never leaves the phone.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, this app data is included in your backup, under your Apple Account. We have no access to it. On Android, if Backup is on in your phone’s settings, Android’s backup service copies your household, watchlist, followed recalls and app settings into your Google Account backup, so they come back when you restore or switch phones. Cached recall lists, photos and alert registration are left out. We have no access to these backups.

2. What leaves your phone, and why

Photos you check

When you check a product, the app asks for permission before sending a photo to Google Gemini through SafeBite’s protected Firebase/Google Cloud server to read its label. You can choose to read it on your phone only instead; this may recognize fewer foods. You can withdraw permission in the app’s Privacy & terms screen. If you agree, the photo is resized, stripped of location and camera metadata, and sent through SafeBite’s protected Firebase/Google Cloud server to Google. SafeBite processes the photo in memory and does not store it. Recognized product details are available for retry for 24 hours so an interrupted request can be recovered without another AI call. After that, they are inaccessible to the app and removed by scheduled cleanup. If Google’s service cannot be reached, the photo is read on your phone instead. Google processes the photo under its own terms and privacy policy, linked below; we do not control how long Google retains it.

On Android, this choice appears once cloud reading is available for your phone; until then every photo is read on your phone and none is sent to SafeBite or to Google. On-device reading on Android uses Google’s ML Kit, which runs on the phone through Google Play services; Google Play services downloads its reading models to the phone. On iPhone, on-device reading uses Apple’s built-in tools.

Alerts

To deliver alerts, an iPhone registers with Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; an Android phone registers with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. That creates a device token and a Firebase installation ID. Your phone then joins alert groups that are named after:

  • your state, and any extra states you add;
  • the allergens in your household;
  • whether you have a pet;
  • the kinds of food and the brands you watch;
  • recalls you follow (as a short code).

Google’s messaging service holds those group memberships against the installation ID. These preferences, including allergen and state selections, are linked to this app installation. They are not attached to a name, an email address or a SafeBite account, because we never have one. Names, ages, barcodes and photos are never part of a group name. We send one message to each group a recall concerns; we never see which phones are in a group and keep no list of devices.

On iPhone, if you choose “Not now” when SafeBite offers alerts, the app still registers for quiet delivery so that an alert about your own Kitchen can appear silently in Notification Center, where iOS asks whether you want to keep receiving them. On Android, the phone joins alert groups only while SafeBite is allowed to show notifications; on Android 13 and later that starts when you allow them, and “Not now” asks nothing. You can turn every group off in Settings → Alerts, or turn notifications off for SafeBite in your phone’s settings (iOS Settings or Android Settings).

Anonymous alert counts

When an alert arrives, and when you open one, the app sends our server three values: whether it arrived or was opened, a short code for the recall, and a reason category (“kitchen”, “allergy”, “pets”, “near” or “none”). No device identifier, cookie, name or household detail is included, and the values are stored without your IP address. We use the totals to see whether alerts are working. They are kept for about 30 days.

Proving the app is genuine

Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from a real copy of SafeBite on a real device: on iPhone it uses Apple’s App Attest or DeviceCheck; on Android it uses Google Play Integrity. This produces a device-level attestation handled by Apple and Google (on iPhone) or by Google (on Android). It checks the app and device without asking for your name, Apple Account or Google Account.

Downloading recalls and pictures

The app downloads the public recall list and its pictures from our server, which runs on Firebase and Google Cloud. A small number of pictures load directly from the websites that publish them, including fda.gov, openfoodfacts.org and retailer image servers. As with any website, those servers and Google see your IP address when your phone connects. Our server code does not store or log it. Google may process connection information under its own privacy policy, linked below.

Visiting these policy pages

These privacy, terms and support pages are hosted by Firebase Hosting. Google processes connection information under its Privacy Policy. Older links on GitHub Pages remain available during the transition and are subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The app does not load these pages unless you choose to open a policy or support link.

Purchases

SafeBite+ subscriptions and tips are billed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. We never receive your payment details or your Apple Account or Google Account email or password. For cloud scans on iPhone, our server receives signed app and subscription purchase information and checks subscription status with Apple. For cloud scans on Android, our server receives a hashed device identifier (made on the phone from Android’s app-specific ID; the ID itself never leaves the phone) and, if you subscribe, your Google Play purchase token, and checks subscription status with Google Play. When you subscribe on Android, a hash tied to that phone is stored with the purchase at Google Play so one subscription cannot be spread across many phones.

The server verifies Firebase App Check and, on iPhone, Apple-signed app and subscription information, or, on Android, the subscription with Google Play, before authorizing a cloud scan. On iPhone, a hashed app purchase identifier links scan counts across devices using the same Apple Account; on Android, free scans are counted per phone and SafeBite+ scans per subscription; SafeBite does not receive your Apple Account email or password. Monthly scan counts and request identifiers are retained for the current and two preceding calendar months, then removed automatically. Android subscription device-sharing records are removed after the current calendar month. Hashed links between successive Google Play subscription purchases are kept while this service operates so changing plans does not reset the shared allowance; these links contain no photos, product details or account names. Clearing local data, reinstalling or changing the phone’s clock does not reset the server allowance.

3. What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No advertising software and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No access to your location, contacts, or photo library. Your state is chosen by hand, and photos you pick are handed to the app one at a time by iOS or by Android’s photo picker. The camera is used only while you check a product, after you allow it.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Deleting your information

Settings → Privacy & terms → “Delete everything on this phone” removes your household, your Kitchen, followed and dealt-with recalls, the check history, your alert settings, pending reminders, and the file shared with the widget and notification components, and it leaves every alert group. Deleting the app removes its ordinary app data. It does not reset the server’s cloud scan allowance or remove the server records described above.

What that cannot reach: copies inside a device backup you made earlier (on iPhone, manage these in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, or on your computer; on Android, in Settings → Google → Backup), and, on iPhone, alerts already showing in Notification Center until you clear them. Server scan records associated with your hashed purchase or device identifier follow the retention period above. Contact us through the support page about access or deletion of those records.

6. Children

SafeBite is meant for the adults who run a household. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A child’s profile is entered by an adult, is stored only on that adult’s phone, and is never sent to us.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses. You can edit or delete local household data in the app, and manage push registration with Apple and Google. For server scan records associated with your purchase or device identifier, contact us through the support page; we will verify your request and explain any limited retention needed to prevent abuse. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS. Information on your phone is protected by iOS or Android and your device passcode or screen lock.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and say so in the app’s release notes or inside the app before the change takes effect.
