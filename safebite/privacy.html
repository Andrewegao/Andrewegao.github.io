SafeBite

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · Qupid, LLC (“we”, “us”)

The short version

  • SafeBite has no accounts. We never receive your name, your email, or the names and ages of the people in your household.
  • Your household, your Kitchen list and your history stay on your phone.
  • Three things may leave your phone: a photo if you choose Google reading (you can read it on-device instead), the alert groups your phone joins (held by Google’s messaging service against an install code, never a name), and an anonymous count when an alert arrives or is opened.
  • No advertising networks, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting software, no location access. We do not sell or share personal information.

1. What stays on your phone

These are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. We cannot see them.

  • Your household: the names you type, whether each profile is an adult, a child or a pet, a child’s age if you enter one, allergens, pet type and your state. An older app version may also have saved an unused alert-strictness answer.
  • Your Kitchen: the products, brands and kinds of food you watch, and a small cropped copy of the photo you added a product with.
  • Recalls you follow and recalls you marked as dealt with.
  • The last 20 checks: the brand, product name and barcode that were read, and any error, kept to help you and us diagnose a failed check. They are never sent to us.
  • Settings such as your alert switches, and a copy of the public recall list and its pictures so the app opens quickly.
  • A small file the app shares with its own widget and notification components on the same phone, holding the household first names, allergens, pet names and watched items, so an alert can say why it is yours. It never leaves the phone.

If you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, this app data is included in your backup, under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

2. What leaves your phone, and why

Photos you check

When you check a product, the app asks for permission before sending a photo to Google (Firebase AI Logic, Gemini) to read its label. You can choose to read it on your phone only instead; this may recognize fewer foods. You can withdraw permission in the app’s Privacy & terms screen. If you agree, the photo is resized, stripped of location and camera metadata, and sent directly from your phone to Google. It never passes through or is stored on our servers. If Google’s service cannot be reached, the photo is read on your phone instead. Google processes the photo under its own terms and privacy policy, linked below; we do not control how long Google retains it.

Alerts

To deliver alerts, your phone registers with Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. That creates a device token and a Firebase installation ID. Your phone then joins alert groups that are named after:

  • your state, and any extra states you add;
  • the allergens in your household;
  • whether you have a pet;
  • the kinds of food and the brands you watch;
  • recalls you follow (as a short code).

Google’s messaging service holds those group memberships against the installation ID. They are not attached to a name, an email address or an account, because we never have one. Names, ages, barcodes and photos are never part of a group name. We send one message to each group a recall concerns; we never see which phones are in a group and keep no list of devices.

If you choose “Not now” when SafeBite offers alerts, the app still registers for quiet delivery so that an alert about your own Kitchen can appear silently in Notification Center, where iOS asks whether you want to keep receiving them. You can turn every group off in Settings → Alerts, or turn notifications off for SafeBite in iOS Settings.

Anonymous alert counts

When an alert arrives, and when you open one, the app sends our server three values: whether it arrived or was opened, a short code for the recall, and a reason category (“kitchen”, “allergy”, “pets”, “near” or “none”). No device identifier, cookie, name or household detail is included, and the values are stored without your IP address. We use the totals to see whether alerts are working. They are kept for about three months.

Proving the app is genuine

Firebase App Check uses Apple’s App Attest or DeviceCheck to confirm that requests come from a real copy of SafeBite on a real device. This produces a device-level attestation handled by Apple and Google. It does not identify you.

Downloading recalls and pictures

The app downloads the public recall list and its pictures from our server, which runs on Cloudflare. A small number of pictures load directly from the websites that publish them, including fda.gov, openfoodfacts.org and retailer image servers. As with any website, those servers and Cloudflare see your IP address when your phone connects. Our server code does not store or log it. Cloudflare may process connection information under its own privacy policy, linked below.

Visiting these policy pages

These privacy, terms and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes under its own Privacy Statement. The app does not load these pages unless you choose to open a policy or support link.

Purchases

SafeBite+ subscriptions and tips are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. We receive only what StoreKit tells the app on your device: whether a purchase is active.

3. What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No advertising software and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No access to your location, contacts, or photo library. Your state is chosen by hand, and photos you pick are handed to the app one at a time by iOS.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Deleting your information

Settings → Privacy & terms → “Delete everything on this phone” removes your household, your Kitchen, followed and dealt-with recalls, the check history, your alert settings, pending reminders, and the file shared with the widget and notification components, and it leaves every alert group. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored on the phone.

What that cannot reach: copies inside a device backup you made earlier (manage these in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, or on your computer), and alerts already showing in Notification Center until you clear them. Because we hold no information that identifies you, there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side.

6. Children

SafeBite is meant for the adults who run a household. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A child’s profile is entered by an adult, is stored only on that adult’s phone, and is never sent to us.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain uses. Because SafeBite keeps your information on your own device and we hold nothing that identifies you, you exercise these rights directly in the app (edit or delete) and with Apple and Google for the push registration. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS. Information on your phone is protected by iOS and your device passcode.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and say so in the app’s release notes or inside the app before the change takes effect.
